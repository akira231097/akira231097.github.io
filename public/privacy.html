Privacy

Portfolio analytics

I use Microsoft Clarity to understand which parts of this portfolio are useful and where the experience needs improvement. It provides page activity, approximate location, device information, scrolling, clicks, and session recordings. Reports are private.

The site's integration denies analytics and advertising cookie storage. It does not set its own visitor identifiers, use fingerprinting, or ask you to sign in. Browsers sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals do not load this analytics integration. An earlier recorded analytics decline is also respected.

Outreach links

A link shared in an outreach message may include campaign labels or a random message code. I may keep a private record connecting that code to the message I sent and use link activity to understand my outreach. Names and email addresses are not placed in these codes or sent as custom visitor identifiers. A link can be forwarded or opened by security software, so its activity does not establish who visited.

Contact

Email and LinkedIn links let you contact me voluntarily. Opening a contact link is not proof that a message was sent. For questions about this portfolio or its analytics, email sarath231097@gmail.com.
